
Evoker Fang Rework(Neoforge Mod)
=======
This Simple Mod Reworked Evoker's Fang Attack.
Inspired by Minecraft Dungeons 2's Evoker

What Evoker Fang Rework Do?
============
When Evoker Fang is summoned, ground particles will appear at its location, making it easier to dodge.

In addition, adjustments have been made to the Evoker, adding two new attack patterns when it summons Evoker Fangs.

If the player approaches the Evoker immediately after its normal attack of summoning Evoker Fangs in a line, it will immediately perform an area-of-effect attack.
